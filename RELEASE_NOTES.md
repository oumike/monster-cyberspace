### Monster for Cyberspace 1.3.4

### Tanmatsu

- The **A** LED (middle right) blinks for new C-Mail and the **B** LED (bottom right) for Feed notifications, each a double blink in its own colour, with the same light timeout and brightness. The messages LED now blinks for the last room and mentions only.
- In Settings and web config, the C-Mail and Feed colours are called **A light (C-Mail)** and **B light (Feed)**; picking a colour blinks that LED once.

### Rooms

- On wide screens with a keyboard (the Tanmatsu), the rooms list has two columns, each room's online count right beside its name. Up and Down move a row, Left and Right a column; Left from the left column goes Home, Right from the right column opens the room.
