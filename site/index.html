Monster for Cyberspace

Firmware for cyberspace.online, flashed from the browser.
This browser can't flash over USB. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Flashing needs a secure (https) page.
Plug the board in over USB, pick it above and press Install. Choosing erase clears saved Wi-Fi, sign-in and settings; without it they are kept. After this, updates come over the air (Settings → Firmware).

Images come from the latest release. Source and docs: github.com/oumike/monster-cyberspace.
