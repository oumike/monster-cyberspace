### Monster for Cyberspace (next release)

### Search

- New **Search** on Home, right after Unread: search cyberspace.online for people, entries and replies. Type a query and press Enter; nothing is sent until you do. Results come in **People**, **Entries** and **Replies** groups, and a group with 8 hits ends in **More…**, which opens a longer list that loads more as you scroll. Open a hit to see the profile, entry or reply; Back returns to your results. Not on the Cardputer ADV.
