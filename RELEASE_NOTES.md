### Monster for Cyberspace (next release)

### Changes

- Enter (Centre) sends what you typed in a room or C-Mail even when the log is scrolled up: it no longer opens the highlighted message's menu while the input line has text. A tap on a message still opens its menu.
- CardputerZero: quitting to the launcher now takes a 3-second hold of Esc (was 1 s), and while you hold it a box shows a bar filling toward quitting with the seconds left. Letting go early goes back as before.
