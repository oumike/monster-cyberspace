### Monster for Cyberspace (next release)

### Slash commands

- In a room or a conversation, typing `/` on an empty line lists the commands with a short hint for each. Keep typing to narrow the list, and pick one to fill it in. Nothing is sent until you send it.
- The commands button (M9 Sym, Tanmatsu Tab, a hold on touch) now opens the room menu. The symbol tray's **commands…** types `/`.
- In C-Mail, `/help` now shows the server's reply in a banner, as it does in rooms.

### Programs

- New **Programs** on Home: browse programs published on cyberspace.online, newest first, and read one's source with its name, author, runtime, release and description. Scroll sideways with Left/Right, or pick **Wrap long lines**. **Releases…** opens older releases, and **Author's profile** opens the author's profile. Read only: nothing runs or installs. A wasm program shows only its size, and source past 32 KB is cut off. Not on the Cardputer ADV.

### Fixes

- The on-screen keyboard now spans the full screen width in every orientation and text size. On the Wio Tracker L2 in portrait it was narrower than the screen.
