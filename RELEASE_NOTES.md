### Monster for Cyberspace 1.3.3

### Rooms

- On touch screens, the rooms list puts a dot before each room with unread messages.
- Which rooms you've read is remembered across restarts: a room shows as unread again only when new messages arrive.

### Emoji

- The emoji tray has 87 emoji, up from 40 (faces, hands, symbols, celebrations, weather and nature, food and drink). When they don't all fit, the tray scrolls.

### Notification light (Mesh Deck, Cyber Deck Plus, Tanmatsu)

- The light now tells apart the last room you opened, mentions in other rooms, Feed notifications and C-Mail. Each one waiting gets a double blink in its own colour, one after another, then a pause.
- Settings and web config have **Channel light** (your old Notify light colour, Blue by default), **Mention light** (Yellow), **Feed light** (Green) and **C-Mail light** (Magenta), each a colour or Off. Each stops when read, or the light timeout after its own last message.

### Web config

- Sections are reorganised (Screen, Lights and sound, ...), with a section list on wide screens, and the page wears the device's theme.
- Changes are collected and sent together with **Save changes** (or Ctrl/⌘+S), or put back with **Discard**. One bad field saves nothing and says which field and why. Leaving with unsaved changes asks first.
