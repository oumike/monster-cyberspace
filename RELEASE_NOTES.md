### Monster for Cyberspace (next release)

### Edit your entries and replies

- **Edit** now appears in the menu of your own entries and replies for 5 minutes after you post them. Entries open in the Composer and replies in the Entry screen's input line. The API lets only supporters edit.

### More in Unread

- Unread now also shows **new followers**, **pokes**, **bookmarks** of your entries, and **new entries** by people you follow. Choosing a follower or a poke opens that person's profile and marks the notice read.
- A new **Unread shows** setting in Settings and web config picks **All** or **Replies & mentions**. Backups save it.

### For contributors

- New `CONTRIBUTING.md`, plus a CI check of pull requests against it.
