### Monster for Cyberspace (next release)

### Changes

- CardputerZero desktop window: Settings → Zoom gains **Auto**, now the default. It draws bigger as the window grows, keeping at least 1280x720 of the app's pixels: 1x on a full-screen 1080p monitor, 2x at 1440p, 3x at 4K. 1x, 2x and 3x still fix the size. A Zoom saved before this release stays as it was; pick Auto in Settings or web config to use it.
- CardputerZero desktop window: Home shows the status lines and your unread items between its button columns, as on the Tab5, instead of leaving most of a big window empty. Settings no longer repeats the status lines there. On the LCD nothing changes.
